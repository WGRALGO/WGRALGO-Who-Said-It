# Privacy Statement

**App:** WGRALGO Who Said It? — Knowledge Is the Weapon™ Edition
**Publisher:** WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc.
**Version:** 1.0.0

## What the app does

The app is an offline educational quote-recognition game. All questions, answers, scores, and feedback are stored only in the app's local memory while you play. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## What the app does **not** do

- No account required
- No ads
- No analytics
- No trackers
- No data selling
- No cloud upload
- No personal data is collected
- No answers or scores are sent to WGRALGO
- No advertising or marketing identifiers used
- No third-party SDKs that phone home

## Android permissions

The app declares **no** dangerous permissions and **no** `INTERNET` permission. It cannot make network requests. The only permission present in the manifest is the auto-generated `DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION` Android adds to apps targeting modern API levels — it is internal to the app and does not grant any access to user data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone of any age, because it does not collect information at all.
