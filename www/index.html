How to check a quote before you share it
  1. Search the exact words in quotation marks and look for the original book, speech, letter, or interview.
  2. Check a quote-research site like Quote Investigator or Wikiquote, which track where quotes really come from.
  3. Be suspicious of quote images with no source. A famous name and a nice background don't make a quote real.
  4. Notice the wording. Modern-sounding phrasing from someone who lived centuries ago is often a clue.
Sources reflect the best-documented origins known to quote researchers. Translations of older works vary.
