How It Works

  • Read the quote on screen.
  • Choose who you think said it from four thinkers.
  • You play 10 randomized rounds per game.
  • Each question shows a Wisdom Category and a Difficulty badge.
  • After you answer, you see Correct or Incorrect, the real speaker, and a short note explaining the quote.
  • Score climbs by 1 per correct answer. End screen shows your final rating.
  • This is an educational quote-recognition game. It teaches recognition, reflection, and attribution awareness — not propaganda or a single ideology.
  • Many famous “quotes” online are misattributed. The point is to learn to check sources.
